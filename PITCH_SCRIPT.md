## 💡 Judge Q&A Defense Sheet (Instant Answers)

### Q1: "How does this differ from tools like Jobscan or Teal?"
> **Answer:** *"Jobscan and Teal are passive keyword counters that encourage keyword stuffing, which human recruiters reject immediately. CareerPulse AI is a closed-loop multi-agent system: it rewrites accomplishments into Google XYZ metrics, conducts live voice interviews on the candidate's exact gaps, and deploys a live web portfolio."*

### Q2: "What prevents candidates from submitting prompt injection attacks in resumes?"
> **Answer:** *"All candidate inputs pass through our Ingestion Prompt-Injection Sanitizer before reaching Gemini, stripping out hidden text delimiters, 'ignore previous instructions', and system overrides."*

### Q3: "What if the user's internet is slow or they don't have a microphone?"
> **Answer:** *"The interview room has full dual-mode support: candidates can seamlessly toggle between live Web Speech voice recognition and a fast text-mode fallback. Furthermore, all 5 agents have heuristic fallbacks that ensure 100% uptime with zero 500 errors."*
